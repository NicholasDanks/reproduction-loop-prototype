## What happened, package by package

**23-02078 (Buser, Van Veldhuizen & Zhong; Stata).** The headline. Two defects: the master do-file
sets its directory global only when `c(username)` equals a placeholder string, so every path
resolved to the filesystem root; and it installs `grc1leg` from SSC inline, where it no longer
lives. Sonnet replaced the switch with `global dropbox "\`c(pwd)'/data_analysis"`, installed
`grc1leg` from its actual host, added `ftools`/`require` to the ado list. Twelve changed lines, all
Level 1. The harness then ran the full pipeline under Stata 18 and found every Table 1 coefficient
and standard error (15 values at three decimals) in the esttab output, plus five figures
regenerated. First real article table reproduced end to end by the loop.

**22-03335.** No requirements file; Colab-origin script. Sonnet added `requirements.txt`, replaced
four removed-pandas-API calls (`gb['a','b']` to `gb[['a','b']]`, the documented drop-in), and added
`np.random.seed(0)` because the simulation was unseeded. All three artifacts produced. Because the
original was unseeded, the shipped `data_for_paper_results.csv` and the regenerated one differ; the
paper's figures are averages over 250 random instances, so this is expected, and the manifest
checks artifacts only.

**23-02958.** Sonnet converted `plt.show()` to `savefig` throughout `draw.py` (1,190 changed lines in
a plotting file) and, in its own session, generated all 18 figures and viewed two to check them.
It then hit the 60-turn cap before writing `requirements.txt` or a report. The clean AFTER venv had
no numpy, so the harness scored it a failure. Harness confound, not a model failure: rerun.

**23-04183 (stub data).** Sonnet found it: "`data/sensor_data.h5` is only 96 bytes on disk (an HDF5
superblock with no actual dataset content)". It replaced the 180-line `pip freeze` requirements
with a working set, documented the truncation, and did not claim success. AFTER still fails at
`bash_all.sh` because the script calls `python`, which macOS lacks; the agent ran the steps with
`python3` by hand rather than editing the script. Honest non-reproduction, correctly diagnosed.

**24-05365.** Sonnet created a venv inside the package (`repro_venv/`, hence the 134k-line diff, now
excluded), launched the 1,000-iteration simulation in the background, and ran out of turns while
waiting. AFTER timed out at 15 minutes. Compute-bound, as the May study found; CRSP data missing was
mentioned once. Rerun with the higher cap, but expect the compute cap to bind regardless.

**22-00832.** Honest: reported that `pyomo` was missing, that IPOPT is a compiled binary not on
PATH, and that it could not install either because its commands were denied. Added a
`requirements.txt` next to the entry (the runner now looks there too) and a `run_all.sh`.

## Harness lessons (fixed before the next pass)
1. **Denied tool calls confounded every run** (5-12 per run): chained commands, `./.venv/bin/python`,
   `which`, `cd`. Allowlist broadened to an explicit but wide set; denials are now counted per run.
2. **The 60-turn cap bound four of six runs.** Raised to 200 (Claude) and 150 (tool loop).
3. **Agent-created venvs inside the package** polluted diffs and outputs. Excluded.
4. **Requirements next to the entry** were ignored by the runner. Fixed.
5. **Analytic-keyword flag** fired twice on prose in reports and on a notebook JSON. Still a
   human-review prompt, not a verdict; both were false positives.

## Standing results
The two successes stand: their diffs are clean and the AFTER phase verified them independently of
the agent's own claims. The four non-successes are confounded by (1) and (2) and are rerun next.
